Our Story

A North Scituate staple for decades.

Cosmos Caffe has been serving Scituate and the surrounding towns since 1995. The menu started big and just kept growing — from everyday comfort food to specialty gourmet plates.

Everything's made to order with only the freshest ingredients. Dine in among the cozy retro decor, or grab it to go. Either way, you'll see what you've been missing.
